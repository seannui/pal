Basis of dispute

I recognize Webflow and authorized an earlier annual subscription. I am not reporting card theft or an unrecognized merchant. I dispute the September 25, 2026 automatic renewal because Webflow did not give the timely, accurate annual renewal notice required for this Colorado business transaction, and its cancellation path does not appear to satisfy Colorado's online cancellation requirements.

Applicable Colorado requirements

C.R.S. § 6-1-732(4)(b) requires a seller to send an annual automatic-renewal notice at least 25 and no more than 40 days before each renewal. Webflow's site-specific notice came seven days before renewal.

C.R.S. § 6-1-732(2)(d) requires a simple, cost-effective, timely, easy-to-use, and readily accessible cancellation mechanism and identifies a one-step online cancellation link as compliant when the consumer enrolled online. Subsection (2.5) permits a seller to display information about cancellation effects or a retention offer only if a prominently located and continuously proximate direct cancellation link is displayed at the same time. The current Webflow path requires the user to choose a downgrade to Starter, pass a consequences screen that lacks a direct final-cancel link, continue to another screen, select a reason, and then confirm the downgrade.

Colorado's final fiscal note for SB 25-145 states that the law's application to businesses took effect February 16, 2026 and applies to contracts offered or renewed on or after that date. Superset operates in Colorado, and this contract renewed on September 25, 2026. Webflow is not among the regulated-industry exemptions listed in C.R.S. § 6-1-732(5).

Please reverse the full $300 charge. We do not seek the renewed September 25, 2026–September 25, 2027 term and have asked Webflow to cancel it and stop future renewal.
